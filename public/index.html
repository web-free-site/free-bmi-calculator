Frequently asked questions

Is Free BMI Calculator free?

Yes. Calculations run entirely in your browser with no account and no server upload of health data.

What BMI categories are used for adults?

Adults (20+) use fixed cutoffs: underweight below 18.5, normal 18.5–24.9, overweight 25–29.9, obesity 30 and above. These do not apply to children.

How is child BMI interpreted?

For ages 0–19, BMI-for-age is compared to WHO growth references by sex and age, showing z-score, percentile, and WHO-based nutritional status—not adult cutoffs.

Is this official WHO software?

No. This site is not official WHO software and is not endorsed by WHO.

Is BMI a medical diagnosis?

No. BMI is an informational estimate only. Consult a qualified health professional.
