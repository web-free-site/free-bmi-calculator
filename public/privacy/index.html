Privacy

Free BMI Calculator processes height, weight, and dates in your browser. We do not operate a backend that receives or stores your health measurements.

Local storage

If you use calculation history, entries are saved in your device’s localStorage only. You can clear them with the Clear control or your browser settings.

Analytics & advertising

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics and third-party advertising scripts that may collect usage data per their own policies. No personally identifiable health data is intentionally transmitted by our calculator logic.
